Shear genius: here's how to keep your chair booked so solid clients can't find an open slot.

This is the Triple Chair Method.

Step 1 is Get Found. Clients only book barbers they can actually find, so the first thing a new client sees has to say who you are, where you are and what you cut.

Step 2 is Fill the Slot. Get them from "nice cut" to booked in as few taps as possible. Every extra back-and-forth DM is a chance for them to book somebody else.

Step 3 is the Rebook. This is where the money's made. A first-time client is one cut. A rebook is a regular.

Get Found, Fill the Slot, the Rebook. That triple combination keeps the chair full.
